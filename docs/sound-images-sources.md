# Sound-card image sources

The 30 sound-card images are original visual assets generated for this project with Manus image generation on 2026-09-05. They were not downloaded from YouTube, TikTok, films, games, applications, or third-party stock libraries. No third-party copyright or stock license is being asserted for these generated assets; they are project-created illustrations intended for embedding in this Android application. Each image is paired one-to-one with the corresponding `licensed_XX_*.wav` item in `SoundsScreen.kt`.

| # | Image | Represents |
|---:|---|---|
| 1–10 | `sound_01_*.png` through `sound_10_*.png` | Police and emergency sirens, beacons, vehicle arrival |
| 11–20 | `sound_11_*.png` through `sound_20_*.png` | Car start, ignition, engine, drive-by, doors, keys, horn |
| 21–25 | `sound_21_*.png` through `sound_25_*.png` | Police radio static, signals, transmission, button |
| 26–30 | `sound_26_*.png` through `sound_30_*.png` | Alerts, emergency tones, police whistles |

The images contain no third-party logos, brand names, or copied characters. They are packaged locally in `app/src/main/res/drawable/` and do not depend on temporary URLs at runtime.
